## Purpose

These bilingual workbooks provide a practical threat map and defensive guidance for employees who use generative AI, enterprise copilots, plugins, connectors, agents, and third-party AI services in office workflows.

They are designed for security awareness, internal policy communication, training, risk discussions, and the development of employee-facing AI usage guidance.

## Content

- Dashboard summarizing the overall threat distribution and risk levels
- Forty practical AI-related office threats
- Threat categories, office examples, severity, likelihood, risk score, and risk level
- Defensive actions that employees can apply immediately
- Employee golden rules for safer AI use
- Source links supporting the risk and control guidance
- English and Hebrew versions for broader organizational adoption

## Covered Risk Areas

The workbooks address topics such as:

- Sensitive-data and source-code exposure
- Shadow AI and unapproved tools
- Prompt injection and malicious instructions
- Unsafe plugins, connectors, skills, and MCP integrations
- Agent permissions and unauthorized automated actions
- Fraud, business email compromise, deepfakes, and identity abuse
- Hallucinations, unreliable outputs, and decision errors
- Intellectual-property, privacy, legal, and compliance risks
- Dangerous code, files, links, commands, and macros
- Third-party services, memory, collaboration, and data segregation
- Cost, availability, bias, governance, and knowledge-integrity risks

## Employee Golden Rules

1. Use only approved enterprise AI tools, plugins, connectors, skills, and agents.
2. Do not enter sensitive, personal, legal, financial, customer, credential, or source-code data unless explicitly approved.
3. Verify AI-generated information before using it in decisions, documents, payments, customer communications, or legal work.
4. Independently validate requests involving money, bank details, credentials, permissions, suppliers, or urgent action.
5. Report suspicious AI outputs, deepfake content, unusual integrations, unexpected data exposure, and prompt-injection behavior.

## Intended Users

- Office employees and managers
- Security-awareness and training teams

- HR, legal, compliance, procurement, and risk functions

## Important Note

The risk scores are intended for awareness and training. They do not replace a formal organizational risk assessment, legal review, privacy impact assessment, or system-specific security evaluation.
